The Conditioning Atlas

Every priming / conditioning experiment in the marine literature, one row per experimental contrast. Built to answer directly: has anyone primed at this stage in a bivalve, and did it stick past metamorphosis?

Source of truth: data/atlas.csv · schema: data/schema/controlled_vocab.yaml. Grain: one row = one experimental contrast (stressor × dose × generation × assay × outcome).
